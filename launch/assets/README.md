## Export notes

- Preserve the full viewBox; do not crop the 96–112 px safe margins.
- Keep text editable in source. Exported raster files are derivatives, not source replacements.
- Do not replace empty capture areas with mock interfaces or third-party screenshots.
- Check every exported image at full size and at a narrow social-feed preview.
- Resolve final channel requirements and public links immediately before upload.
